Man Went Out for Water and Ended Up Finding a Three-Headed Dinosaur

The strange trip ended with a zombie, a famous actress and a soup being cooked inside a pool filled with billiard balls.

By: The What? Verses Newsroom Reading time: 2 minutes Status: Nobody Knows
Reconstruction of the events according to people who were there

On February 39, a man opened his refrigerator to get a glass of water from the beach that he had inside. When he looked at his table, there was no bread, so he told his wife to go get some.

On the way, the woman found a three-headed dinosaur. Since it was small, she felt sorry for leaving it and took it home.

When they arrived, the bread was left on the table, and she told her husband to go to the backyard to see what she had brought. When they got there, all the plants had been eaten by the dinosaur, so they both ran away.

When the dinosaur saw the man, it got scared and ran away too. The worried woman followed it until she reached a zombie's house.

“The soup was not in a pot. It was being cooked directly in the pool.” — The woman's statement

When she arrived, the most famous actress in Hollywood was found making soup directly in a pool filled with billiard balls. According to the actress, the pot was occupied because the zombies were having a party with it.

The situation became even stranger when the dinosaur got into the soup and said that it was very cold.

So far, nobody knows why a three-headed dinosaur, a zombie, an actress and a pool filled with billiard balls were all in the same place.
